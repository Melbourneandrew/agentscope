## Overview

Agentscope begins with Langfuse and must grow to additional observability destinations without changing how agent harnesses capture work.

## Terminology

- **Idempotency key:** a stable delivery identity that prevents duplicate trace export.

## Requirements

### REQ-REP-001: Reliable delivery

As a user, I want captures delivered reliably without slowing my agent, so that traces are useful even during transient destination failures.

- **AC-REP-001.1:** When a delivery is retryable, the system shall queue a bounded redacted batch and retry with idempotency.
- **AC-REP-001.2:** When delivery is non-retryable or storage limits are reached, the system shall retain a sanitized diagnostic and fail open.

### REQ-REP-002: Destination extensibility

As an Agentscope contributor, I want a stable reporter contract, so that I can add a first-party destination without modifying harness adapters.

- **AC-REP-002.1:** When a reporter receives a batch, the system shall supply normalized and redacted trace data independent of source harness format.
