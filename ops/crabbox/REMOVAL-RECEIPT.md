# Protected launcher removal receipt

Date: 2026-09-02

The custom protected launcher was removed completely. The removal covered its
repository source, installer and upgrade scripts, CI contract, policy scripts
and tests, fleet skill, coordinator templates and operational ceremony. The
user-owned legacy wrapper and its two launcher-specific Keychain credentials
were removed. No launcher service or root-owned launcher installation was
present. The upstream Crabbox v0.46 Homebrew installation remains as the
replacement client.

No credential values, account identifiers, provider resource identifiers,
addresses, prompts, or terminal payloads are retained in this receipt.
